Shift Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Shift Manager ("the app") helps hourly workers record their working hours and work out their pay. It is made by Kevs Studio.

What stays on your phone

Your shifts, pay settings, schedules, paid leave, workplace outline and settings are saved on your phone only. We do not have a server and we do not receive this data. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. If you use "Back up", the file is saved where you choose.

Location

If you switch on Auto Mode, the app uses your phone's precise location, also when the app is closed or not in use, to notice when you enter or leave the workplace area you drew. This is how it clocks you in and out. Your location is compared with your workplace area on your phone. Auto Mode does not send it to us or to anyone else. A notification shows while Auto Mode is watching. You can switch Auto Mode off in Settings, or take back the location permission in your phone's settings, at any time.

Map and place search

The map is provided by Google Maps. When you search for a workplace, the app sends Google the words you typed, the position of the middle of the map, and the place you pick. If you centred the map on your own position first, that position is your exact location. The search is optional: you can place your workplace on the map by hand instead. Google's privacy policy applies to that: policies.google.com/privacy.

The Google Maps library inside the app also sends Google some technical data when the map is shown: details about your phone (system version, name and model), the version of the app and of the library, your phone's internet address, crash information, and a generated identifier. Google uses it to run and improve its services. We do not receive this data.

Public holidays

Depending on the region you choose in the app, it downloads a list of public holidays for Japan, the Philippines or Singapore from a public website. No personal data is sent with it, but the website can see your phone's internet address, as any website can.

What we do not do

The app itself has no analytics of its own and no accounts, and we do not sell your data. The data that leaves your phone is described above, in the sections on the map and on public holidays, and below, in the section on ads.

The About page of the app links to our Facebook page and to our contact email. If you open the Facebook link, Facebook's own privacy policy applies there; the app itself sends nothing to Facebook.

Ads

From version 3.1 the app shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob: on the Today screen after you clock out, and on the Calendar screen. On the map screen, once you have used your free place searches for the day, the app offers an optional short video ad from Google AdMob that gives you more searches; nothing is shown unless you choose to watch it. The ads are not chosen by your interests: the app asks Google for non-personalised ads. Google's ad software still receives some technical data to show and measure the ads and to prevent fraud: your phone's internet address (which can hint at a rough location), how the ads are viewed and tapped, performance and crash information, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID. The app does not pass your shifts, your pay or your GPS location to the ad software.

Where the law requires it (the European Union, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states) the app first asks for your choice, and you can change it later in Settings, "Ad privacy choices". You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads: policies.google.com/privacy.

Rating the app

After you have used the app for a while, Google Play may show its own rating sheet inside the app. If you choose to rate, the stars and any text you write go to Google Play, not to us; the app does not receive them.

Children

The app is for working people and is not aimed at children.
