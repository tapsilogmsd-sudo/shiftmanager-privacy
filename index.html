Shift Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Shift Manager ("the app") helps hourly workers record their working hours and work out their pay. It is made by Kevs Studio.

What stays on your phone

Your shifts, pay settings, schedules, paid leave, workplace outline and settings are saved on your phone only. We do not have a server and we do not receive this data. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. If you use "Back up", the file is saved where you choose.

Location

If you switch on Auto Mode, the app uses your phone's precise location, also when the app is closed or not in use, to notice when you enter or leave the workplace area you drew. This is how it clocks you in and out. Your location is compared with your workplace area on your phone. Auto Mode does not send it to us or to anyone else. A notification shows while Auto Mode is watching. You can switch Auto Mode off in Settings, or take back the location permission in your phone's settings, at any time.

Map and place search

The map is provided by Google Maps. When you search for a workplace, the app sends Google the words you typed and the area the map is showing, and the place you pick. Google's privacy policy applies to that: policies.google.com/privacy.

Public holidays

The app downloads a list of public holidays from a public website. No personal data is sent with it, but the website can see your phone's internet address, as any website can.

What we do not do

No ads, no analytics, no accounts, and we do not sell or share your data.

Children

The app is for working people and is not aimed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
